## 🔧 **Configuration Required**

### **Google Analytics**
Replace `GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` in `app/layout.js` with your actual Google Analytics ID:
```javascript
gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX'); // Replace with your GA4 ID
```

### **Search Console Verification**
Update verification codes in `app/layout.js`:
```javascript
verification: {
  google: 'your-google-verification-code',
  yandex: 'your-yandex-verification-code',
  yahoo: 'your-yahoo-verification-code',
},
```

### **Domain Configuration**
Update all URLs from `decorsdigital.com` to your actual domain in:
- `app/layout.js`
- `app/sitemap.js`
- `public/robots.txt`
- `app/constants/index.js`
